Free, clean water for rural Uganda.

Qalb Kareem Foundation builds solar-powered boreholes, community mosques and safe places of learning, and hands each one to the community to run for generations.

Partner with us See our work

Three pillars, one community

Water comes first because nothing else works without it. Around it we build the places where people gather, and the classrooms where children learn.

Clean water access (WASH)

Solar boreholes, free to everyone

We drill deep wells and power them with solar, so families no longer walk for unsafe water. Every borehole is a free public water point with no fee and no barrier, cutting the waterborne illness that hits children hardest.

Community infrastructure

Hubs and mosques that hold people together

We build and maintain regional hubs and community mosques that serve as centres for relief, unity and resilience.

Faith and education

Safe classrooms, Quranic learning

We secure structured learning spaces and materials so local youth grow up with strong values and quality Quranic education.

Built to outlast us

A well that breaks after two years helps no one. So every project is owned by the people it serves.

We work to strict international standards of transparency, and each water point is managed by a Water Users Committee drawn from the community itself. That keeps projects physically and operationally sustainable for generations.

  1. ListenVillages tell us where need is greatest.
  2. BuildWe engineer the borehole, solar system or building to a high standard.
  3. Hand overA Water Users Committee takes charge of care and upkeep.
  4. Stay accountableWe report openly on what was built and how it is running.
